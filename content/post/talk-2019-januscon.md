**Designing a Better Real-Time Media Control Protocol for a Smarter World**, by Maksym Sobolyev, at JanusCon 2019.

The RTPProxy has seen quite a lot of adoption in the telco world in the last 15 years, in a big part thanks to some of the key decisions around designing and extending its control protocol. There are some challenges that require a near-complete overhaul to provide for previously unforeseen scenarios.
