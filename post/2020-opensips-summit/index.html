Talk: Sippy Labs Update - RTPProxy, OpenSIPIt and Microsippy

  2020-09-10

Sippy Labs Update - RTPProxy, OpenSIPIt and Microsippy, by Maksym Sobolyev, at OpenSIPS Summit 2020 (online).

An overview of the latest activities at Sippy in the open-source SIP space, covering:

  • RTPProxy 2.2: what’s new? Road towards RTPProxy 3.0;
  • Microsippy (very small-scale SIP): where are we, what’s next?;
  • OpenSIPIt 2020: “Stirring but not Shaking”. Open SIP interoperability testing, from idea to implementation.
