Talk: Scaling RTPProxy

  2015-12-09

Scaling RTPProxy, by Maksym Sobolyev (@sobomax) and Jev Bjorsell (@jevonearth), at the OpenSIPS developer summit in Austin, Texas.

A look at scaling RTPProxy: past, present and future. The talk focuses on the RTPProxy, our open-source VoIP media proxy/media relay, and also covers our rtp_cluster product.
